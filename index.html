How to play

  1. Player X moves first.
  2. Take turns placing your symbol in an empty square.
  3. Three in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins.
  4. If all nine squares fill with no winner, the game is a draw.
  5. You cannot move out of turn or play an occupied square.

Battle rules

  1. X starts the match.
  2. Only the current player can make a move.
  3. Each square can be used once.
  4. Three matching symbols in a row wins.
  5. Nine filled squares without a winner means draw.
  6. The turn timer is 10 seconds. When it expires, the turn is passed to the other player.
